About This Project

An academic Fake News Detection System utilizing a Scikit-Learn TF-IDF vectorizer and Logistic Regression classifier trained on the peer-reviewed WELFake dataset (72,000+ benchmark articles).

Important Notice: Predictions evaluate linguistic patterns and vocabulary distribution. A prediction of "Likely Real" or "Likely Fake" does not prove factual truth or falsehood.
